बोल साथीবোল সাথীBol Saathi

    About this prototype

    The task. Build a working voice agent that helps people learn vocabulary. This one was made for the Apna Advantage Program.

    Who it’s for. People who move to another state for a job. A warehouse worker from Bihar starting in Kolkata, or a delivery partner from Bengal starting in Delhi, needs a handful of words in week one: directions, rent, salary and “please speak slowly”.

    How it works. Pick your language and the language of your new city. Bol Saathi teaches five words per topic in your language, asks you to say each one, checks what it hears, and gives a hint and a second try when needed. It ends with a quick check based on real situations, starting with the words you found hard. Each word appears in its own script, with the pronunciation written in yours.

    Why voice. The goal is to be understood out loud, and many first-time workers are more comfortable speaking than reading.

    How it’s built. Made with Claude as a single web page. It uses the browser’s own speech recognition and voices, so there’s no app, sign-up or paid service. It works best in Chrome or Edge with a microphone.

    What I’d measure. Words recalled correctly the next day, lessons finished, and how often learners ask to hear a word slowly.

    What’s next. Word packs for specific jobs (delivery, retail, hospitality), short daily lessons that bring back missed words, more languages starting with Odia, and a WhatsApp voice version.

    Try it. As a Hindi speaker moving to Kolkata, or as a Bengali speaker moving to Delhi.
